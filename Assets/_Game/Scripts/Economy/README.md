# Economy sample

The Week 6 economy loop and its telemetry. `Wallet.cs` holds coins and level: a tap earns `5 × (level + 1)`, the next upgrade costs `100 × 1.5^level`, and `TryUpgrade` reads the cost before the level rises and logs `upgrade_purchased`. `WalletHud.cs` redraws only when the wallet raises `Changed`. `WalletSave.cs` and `WalletPersistence.cs` keep the wallet in PlayerPrefs, saving on every change and when the app goes to the background, never relying on `OnApplicationQuit`. `IPurchaseProvider.cs`, `FakePurchaseProvider.cs` and `CoinShop.cs` are the store stub: gameplay asks the interface, the fake logs `[iap-stub]` and grants the pack, and a real billing SDK would be one new class plus the one line in `CoinShop` that creates the provider. `Telemetry.cs` in `Scripts/Shared/` writes each event to logcat and to `telemetry.log` in `persistentDataPath`. Copy those. The tests are `WalletTests`, `WalletSaveTests`, `TelemetryTests` and `TapRoundTests` in `Assets/_Game/Editor/Tests/`.

`TapRound.cs` is demo scaffolding standing in for your core verb: a 20 s round of tapping three targets, 15 taps to win, logging `level_start`, `level_complete` and `level_fail`; the shop and Start show only between rounds, and Reset wallet only in development builds. The event map for this sample is `docs/economy-telemetry-map.md`; design notes are in the `MGD_MODULE_GDD` vault, note `Samples/Economy`.
