## Long description (4000 characters max)

MGD Samples is the companion app to the SETU Mobile Game Development module. It is not a game: each scene shows one thing a mobile game must get right, small enough to read in one sitting and copy into your own project.

Open it on your phone, pick a topic, and see the behaviour for yourself before you build it.

What is in this build:

- Safe areas: a HUD that stays clear of the notch and the gesture bar, in both orientations.
- Lifecycle: Home, a call or the screen turning off pauses the scene; you choose when to resume.
- Accessibility: haptics, three text sizes and reduce motion, remembered between sessions.
- Performance and tiers: frame-time readouts under load, and quality levels picked for your phone.
- Touch, pooling and loading: multi-finger drag, a reused object pool, and a loading bar that never hangs.
- Economy: a timed tap round, an upgrade and a pretend store, with every event in a local log.
- Thermal soak: an overlay showing frame time and how warm the phone is getting.
- About and QA: a build stamp for bug reports, and buttons that make an exception, a freeze and a crash on purpose so you can find them in the log.

Works fully offline. No adverts, no real purchases (the store is pretend), no accounts; the app sends nothing anywhere, and progress is saved locally. Android back returns to the list, or opens the pause menu where a sample needs back for itself; there is no Quit button, because Android already has one.

Made for students of A12581 at South East Technological University, academic year 2026/2027.

(1,549 characters)
